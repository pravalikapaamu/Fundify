About Fundify

A Simple Crowdfunding Platform for Social Causes

Fundify is a crowdfunding platform project designed to help users create, explore, and support fundraising campaigns. It focuses on causes like education, healthcare, environment, and community support with a simple and user-friendly interface.

Our Purpose

Making Online Fundraising Easy to Understand

The purpose of Fundify is to provide a clear platform where campaign creators can share their cause and donors can view campaign details before supporting them. The project is built with a focus on clean design, transparency, and easy navigation.

What Fundify Focuses On

The main ideas behind the design and functionality of this project.

Support

Helping campaign creators present their causes clearly so donors can understand why support is needed.

Trust

Providing organized campaign details, goal amounts, progress, and donor information in a clear format.

Community

Connecting people with causes related to healthcare, education, environment, and social welfare.

About This Project

Fundify was developed as an academic major project to demonstrate a front-end crowdfunding platform with campaign listing, campaign details, form validation, and a clean user interface.
